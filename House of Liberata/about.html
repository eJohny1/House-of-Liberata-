#OUR STORY

More than fashion, a legacy

Wear Your Heritage

At House of Liberata, we believe clothing is more than fabric, it's identity. Every boubou, every stitch, every pattern carries the stories of our ancestors and the pride of being Cameroonian.

We exist to ensure that tradition is not just preserved, but worn daily with joy, with confidence, with belonging.

From a Dream in Buea

House of Liberata was born in Clerks Quarters, Buea,from a simple desire, to see Cameroonian women and men wear their culture with pride, not just on special occasions, but every single day.

What started as a small collection of handcrafted boubous for family and friends quickly grew into something bigger. People didn't just want the clothes, they wanted the stories behind them.

Today, we work with master artisans across Cameroon, from Bamenda to Douala to Yaoundé, preserving traditional techniques while creating designs that feel fresh, modern, and ready for the world.

"Liberata means freedom. Freedom to be who you are, to wear where you come from, to walk into any room and know my ancestors are with me." Emilienne, Founder
